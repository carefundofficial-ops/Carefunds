How CareFund works

1. Create & verify

Campaign creators must be 18+ and complete liveness camera verification. Parents/guardians can create campaigns for minors and state their relationship.

2. Donate & verify

Donors submit a TXID. It remains pending until an admin verifies the payment.

3. Withdraw responsibly

Withdrawal becomes available at 60% of the target. A 10% fee applies. Admin reviews and sends funds.

4. Confirm receipt

After payment is sent, the beneficiary has 72 hours to confirm receipt. Registered contributing donors receive campaign updates.

Built around trust 🔐

CareFund separates public campaign information from private donor, identity and payout information. Liveness is used as a verification step and does not by itself release funds.
